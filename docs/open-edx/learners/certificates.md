Once you have successfully completed all course requirements, Open edX automatically generates your digital certificate of completion. Follow this guide to check your eligibility and download your certificate.

## Part 1: Checking Certificate Eligibility

1. **Meet the Passing Criteria:**

   * Ensure you have completed all mandatory assignments, quizzes, and exams, and that your overall grade meets or exceeds the passing threshold set for the course.

2. **Verify Your Progress:**

   * Navigate to your **Dashboard**, open your course, and click on the **"Progress"** tab. 
   * Check that all required components show completed statuses and that your final grade is posted.

## Part 2: Receiving Your Certificate

1. **Automatic Generation:**

   * Once the course team finalizes grades and you meet all criteria, the platform automatically generates your digital certificate. (Note: For some instructor-paced courses, certificates may be issued after the official course end date).

2. **Notification:**

   * You will typically see a notification banner on your course dashboard or receive an email notifying you that your certificate is ready.

## Part 3: Downloading Your Certificate

1. **Go to Your Dashboard:**

   * Log in and navigate to your main **Dashboard**.

2. **Locate the Completed Course:**

   * Find the card for your completed course in the "Completed" section.

3. **Download Options:**

   * Click the **"Download Certificate"** button (available as a PDF) or the **"View Certificate"** link.
   * You can save the PDF to your device, print it, or share the unique certificate URL directly on professional networks like LinkedIn.

*Need help? If you have passed all requirements but do not see your certificate, please contact your course administrator or support team.*
